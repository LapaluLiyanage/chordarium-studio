# Manual Cubase checklist (run on the laptop)

- [ ] Plugin appears after scan and opens without a black window (WebView2 installed).
- [ ] Core starts automatically; the status chip shows Engine ready.
- [ ] Paste an English and a Sinhala sheet; flagged bars are highlighted; fix one chord.
- [ ] Arrange a chorus; preview follows Cubase transport in Follow mode and loops in Free mode.
- [ ] Drag a part onto a track: a MIDI part is created.
- [ ] Print to tracks records 4 parts in one pass.
- [ ] Save and reopen the project: song and parts are restored.
- [ ] pluginval strictness 5 passes. CPU < 5% with 4 parts on built-in sounds.
- [ ] Transport tool from Claude starts/stops Cubase.
